01 / Problem

The paperwork is the hard part.

Italy is the easy decision. The process is where people stall.

  1. 01.1

    Appointments, months out.

    Consulate visa slots in the U.S. can book up far in advance — and you can only apply at the one that covers where you live.

  2. 01.2

    Every consulate, a different list.

    The same visa can come with different document requirements, formats, and proofs depending on which consulate you're assigned.

  3. 01.3

    The lease / codice fiscale loop.

    You need accommodation to apply; landlords often want a codice fiscale. Knowing which comes first — and how to get it from abroad — matters.

  4. 01.4

    Apostilles, translations, insurance.

    Records need apostilles and certified translations, and health insurance that doesn't meet consulate requirements is a common reason to redo an application.
